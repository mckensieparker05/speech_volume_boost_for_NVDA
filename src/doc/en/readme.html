Speech Volume Boost

Amplifies NVDA speech volume for any synthesizer. The add-on applies a gain to NVDA's speech audio stream after the synthesizer has rendered it, so it works with eSpeak NG, OneCore, SAPI5, SAPI4 and third-party synthesizer drivers alike, without touching the synthesizer's own volume setting.

What it does

  • Intercepts every chunk of 16-bit speech PCM that NVDA feeds to its wave player and scales it by a user-configurable gain.
  • Because all synthesizers route their audio through the same wave player, one hook covers them all.
  • Only speech is affected; NVDA sounds, tones and other audio are left untouched.

Using the add-on

Commands

NVDA+alt+g Toggles speech volume boost on or off.
NVDA+alt+shift+g Increases the gain by 10 percent.
NVDA+alt+control+g Decreases the gain by 10 percent.
NVDA+alt+control+shift+g Reports the current state and gain.

All commands can be remapped in NVDA's Input Gestures dialog under the "Speech Volume Boost" category.

Settings

Open NVDA's Settings dialog and choose the "Speech Volume Boost" category to:

  • Enable or disable the boost.
  • Set the gain from 0 to 400 percent. 100 percent means unchanged; 200 percent doubles the loudness and 400 percent quadruples it.

Changes take effect immediately for speech spoken after the change.

Caveats

  • At high gains the signal can reach the maximum 16-bit sample value and clip. If you hear distortion, lower the gain.
  • Audio that is not 16-bit PCM, and any non-speech output, is passed through unmodified.

Compatibility

Requires NVDA 2026.1 or later (64-bit). The add-on is implemented entirely in Python and bundles no binaries, so no 32-bit/64-bit native libraries are involved.
